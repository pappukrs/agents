# Payments

We accept UPI, RuPay, Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards, net banking from major Indian banks, and the Nimbus wallet. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 10,000 rupees.

No-cost EMI is available on credit cards from partner banks for orders above 5,000 rupees, over 3, 6 or 9 months. The bank may charge GST on the interest component.

Payments are processed by our payment gateway; we never see or store your full card number. If money was deducted but the order failed, it is reversed automatically within 5 to 7 business days.

All prices include GST. A GST invoice is emailed when the order is dispatched; business customers can add their GSTIN at checkout.
