# Account security

Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) under Account > Security. You can use an authenticator app or SMS codes. With 2FA on, every new device must enter a code before it can sign in.

Password reset links are sent to your registered email and expire after 30 minutes. If you no longer have access to that email, contact support with an order ID and the last four digits of the card used, so we can verify you.

Nimbus will never ask for your password, OTP or full card number by phone, SMS or email. If someone asks, it's a scam: don't share anything, and report it to security@nimbus.example.

After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. You can sign out of all devices at once from Account > Security > Active sessions.
