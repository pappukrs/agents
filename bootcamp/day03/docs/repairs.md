# Paid repairs

Damage that the warranty doesn't cover can be repaired for a fee at any authorised Nimbus service centre. This includes liquid damage, cracked screens and drop damage.

Book a repair under Support > Repairs. A technician inspects the device and sends a quote within 3 business days. Nothing is repaired until you approve the quote. If you decline, the device is returned and you pay a flat inspection fee of 499 rupees.

Paid repairs carry their own 90-day warranty on the replaced parts. Typical turnaround after you approve the quote is 5 to 7 business days, longer if parts must be ordered.

Data on the device may be erased during repair. Back up your data before sending it in.
