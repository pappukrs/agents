# Shipping

We ship to all serviceable PIN codes in India. We don't ship outside India.

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders above 999 rupees; below that it costs 49 rupees. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days in metro cities and costs 149 rupees regardless of order value. Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day are dispatched the same day.

You'll get a tracking link by SMS and email once the order is dispatched. Large appliances are delivered by a separate courier and may take up to 7 business days.

## Damaged or missing parcels

If a parcel arrives damaged, take photos before opening it fully and report it within 48 hours of delivery under My Orders > Report a problem. Claims after 48 hours can't be accepted for transit damage. If tracking shows delivered but you didn't receive the parcel, report it within 7 days.
