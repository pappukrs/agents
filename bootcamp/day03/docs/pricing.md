# Nimbus Care+ plans

Nimbus Care+ is an optional protection plan you can add when buying a laptop, phone or tablet, or within 30 days afterwards.

- Care+ Basic: 99 rupees per month or 999 rupees per year. Extends the warranty to 24 months and gives priority support.
- Care+ Complete: 199 rupees per month or 1,999 rupees per year. Everything in Basic, plus one accidental-damage repair per year (drops, cracked screens and liquid damage) with a service fee of 999 rupees per claim.

Plans renew automatically and can be cancelled at any time from Account > Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; there is no refund for the current period.

Prices on the website match prices in our partner stores. We don't price-match other retailers.
