# Warranty

All products sold by Nimbus Electronics come with a 12-month warranty from the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects: parts or workmanship that fail under normal use. Laptops and phones bought with a Nimbus Care+ plan get an extended 24-month warranty.

To make a claim, go to Support > Warranty claim and upload the invoice and a short video or photo of the fault. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at no cost; if a repair isn't possible we replace the item with the same or an equivalent model.

## Not covered

The warranty does not cover water or other liquid damage, drops and cracked screens, normal wear such as battery capacity loss below 80% after 12 months, cosmetic scratches, or damage from unauthorised repairs. Opening the device or replacing parts outside an authorised service centre voids the warranty.

Accessories such as cables and cases have a 6-month warranty.
