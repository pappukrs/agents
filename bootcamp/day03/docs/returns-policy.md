# Returns policy

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery, in their original packaging with all accessories, manuals and free gifts included. The 30-day window starts on the delivery date shown in your order history, not the order date.

To start a return, open My Orders, choose the item and select "Return". We email a prepaid return label within 24 hours. Drop the parcel at any partner courier point within 7 days of receiving the label, or the return request expires and you must raise a new one.

## What can't be returned

Opened software, gift cards, earphones and in-ear headphones (for hygiene reasons) and items marked "final sale" can't be returned. Opened items in other categories can be returned only if they are faulty; a change of mind applies to unopened items only.

Items damaged by the customer, or missing their serial-number sticker, are refused at inspection and sent back at the customer's cost.

## Exchanges

We don't do direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order. If the new item is more expensive, you pay the difference as a normal order.
