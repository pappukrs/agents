# Refunds

Refunds are issued after the returned item passes inspection at our warehouse. Inspection takes up to 2 business days from the day the parcel arrives.

Once approved, refunds go to the original payment method within 5 business days. UPI and wallet refunds are usually faster, often the same day. Card refunds can take a further 3 to 7 days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer: add your account details under My Orders > Refund details.

We can't refund to a different card or account from the one you paid with, except for cash-on-delivery orders.

## Partial refunds

If an item comes back with missing accessories or signs of use, we refund the item price minus a restocking fee of 15%. Original shipping charges are refunded only when the item was faulty or we sent the wrong item.

## Cancelled orders

Orders cancelled before dispatch are refunded in full, including shipping, within 2 business days.
